# AFTER HOURS, Day 1: script draft v1

Built on `story-blueprint.md`. Pixel Dread look, new school world. Narrator: user's voice (Chatterbox).
Bed: `scripts/liminal_hum.py` (boxed-in hum). Target ~72 s, ~167 words.

**The threat's one rule:** the infected copy the voices of people you know. Noise draws them in.
**HUD:** `● DAY 1`, clock from 4:52 PM, status `RIVERSIDE HIGH · LIBRARY` → `· NO POWER`. Top right: live
**NOISE** meter that spikes on every sound (alert, PA, ringtone, keys, knock) and falls back slowly.
**Ending:** OPEN THE DOOR / STAY SILENT. End card: AFTER HOURS / DAY 1 / 5:07 PM / FOLLOW FOR PART 2.
