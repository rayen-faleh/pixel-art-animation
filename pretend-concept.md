# KEEP PRETENDING: cat concept B, outline v1

Alternative to `slow-blink-concept.md`. There, the cat is the eerie protector. **Here, the cat is the eerie thing.**
Long form, 16:9 (NIGHT ROAD kit), second person, present tense, ~9–10 min (~1,200 words at ~125 wpm).

**Title:** If You Catch Your Cat Standing Up at Night, Pretend You Didn't See
Alternates: *If Your Cat Does This at 3 AM, Keep Treating It Like a Cat* · *Never Let Your Cat Know You've Seen the Camera*

**Thumbnail:** a grainy night-vision pet-camera frame (generic UI, timestamp `03:12:44`). A dark kitchen. A cat stands
upright on its back legs in the middle of the floor, perfectly straight, its eyes two white IR dots, looking straight
into the lens. Text: **IF YOUR CAT DOES THIS… / PRETEND YOU DIDN'T SEE.**

---

## The idea in one line

Your new cat acts exactly like a cat for as long as you act like it's a cat. The night it finds out you've seen what
it really does, it stops pretending.

## The true fact that flips

- Cat-behaviour researcher John Bradshaw (*Cat Sense*, 2013) argues that **cats don't treat us as people. They treat us
  as big, clumsy cats.** It's a well-known, shareable "aww" fact.
- **The flip:** what if it goes both ways? Your cat has been watching how *you* act, too. "It's been treating you like a
  big, clumsy cat. Tonight it starts treating you like something else."
- Supporting real details (beat chips): night-vision pet cameras make cats' eyes glow white (eyeshine under infrared);
  cats sleep 12–16 hours a day, mostly while you're awake; cats can learn to open lever door handles by jumping.

What the viewer takes away: **if your cat does something strange at night, don't react. Keep treating it like a cat.**
That's harmless and funny to try ("I stared at my cat and pretended nothing was weird"). It works as a comment and share
prompt, and it fits the "rules" format.

## Rule of the threat (never said outright)

**It stays a cat as long as you don't let it know you know.** Every reaction you show (a flinch, a scream, a light
switched on, a second look) pushes it one step closer to dropping the act. The engine is a classic one, used in a new
place: the horror isn't that something is in the house, it's that **you have to keep living with it, normally**.

**Meter (top right):** **SUSPICION**, shown as a cat-eye icon whose pupil narrows. It rises when you react and falls
when you act normal (feed it, pet it, call it by name).
**HUD:** `KEEP PRETENDING · NIGHT n · clock · APT 4C`, chip `PEPPER · ASLEEP / WATCHING / STANDING / ???`.
**Big question:** the shelter card says *"Returned 3 times. No reason given."* Where are the three people who returned him?

## Characters

- **You.** Living alone, newly adopted cat from a shelter.
- **Pepper.** Grey tuxedo cat, a white "bib" and white socks, adult, very calm. The shelter card says
  *Returned 3 times. No reason given.* Always seems to be facing you when you turn around.
- **Dana.** One of the previous adopters, reached through the shelter (texts and one voice note, the user's voice with fx).
- **The shelter volunteer.** One scene.

## Outline

### COLD OPEN (0:00–0:45)
1. You got up for water at three twelve, and your cat was standing in the kitchen.
2. Not on all fours. Standing. Straight up, like a person waiting for something.
3. It was looking at the fridge. Then it turned its head and looked at you.
4. And you did the only thing that saved your life. You said, *"Hey buddy,"* got your water, and went back to bed.
5. This is how you learned the first rule. Pretend you didn't see.

> Cold open is the thumbnail moment in the world, then the hook headline.
> TITLE: KEEP PRETENDING / 3:12 AM.

### 1. RETURNED THREE TIMES (0:45–2:15), two weeks earlier
- The shelter. Pepper is the calmest cat there. The card on his cage: *Returned 3 times. No reason given.*
- The volunteer: "He's perfect. People just… bring him back."
- Fact chip: *cats treat you like a big, clumsy cat.* "He sleeps on your feet. Headbutts you. Purrs when you come home."
- Small wrongnesses, one per beat: he never makes a sound when he jumps down; his food bowl is always empty but you
  never see him eat; whenever you turn around, he's already facing you.
- **Cliff:** a pet cam, bought "to watch him at work". The first clip the app flags: `MOTION · 03:12`.

### 2. THE CAMERA (2:15–4:00)
- Pet-cam inserts (night-vision grain, IR eyes, timestamp, scrub bar). 03:12: Pepper walks into frame on four legs.
  03:13: he sits. 03:14: he **stands up** and walks out of frame on two.
- You scrub forward. 03:20 to 04:05: he stands at your bedroom door, perfectly still, for forty-five minutes.
- 04:06: he turns his head and looks into the camera.
- You don't sleep. In the morning Pepper headbutts your hand, purrs and wants breakfast, like always.
- **Cliff:** the app shows a new notification: `CAMERA MOVED · 04:07`. It's now pointed at your bed.

### 3. DANA (4:00–5:45)
- You call the shelter for the previous adopters. Two numbers are disconnected. One answers by text: Dana.
- Her texts arrive as the rules (rule cards, each after the behaviour it answers):
  1. **"Did he stand up yet? Don't react. Whatever he's doing, he's a cat. Talk to him like a cat."**
  2. **"Don't change anything. Same food, same time, same lights. He notices changes."**
  3. **"Never watch the camera where he can see you watching."**
  4. **"If he's in your spot on the bed, sleep somewhere else and say nothing."**
  5. **"Don't bring him back. When I brought him back, I…"** (the text breaks off).
- Voice note from Dana, whispered, the user's voice with fx: *"He doesn't want to be a cat. He wants to be the one
  who gets fed."*
- **Cliff:** you look up from your phone. Pepper is sitting on the counter, watching the screen over your shoulder.

### 4. THE SPOT (5:45–7:45): set piece
- You follow the rules: same food, same lights, "good boy". The SUSPICION meter stays low.
- Night. You wake up cold. Pepper isn't on your feet. He's on your pillow, in your spot, lying the way you lie.
  Rule 4: you get up without a word and sleep on the sofa.
- From the sofa you can see the pet cam's tiny status light. You must not check the app (rule 3).
- You check the app under the blanket, brightness all the way down.
- On the feed: your bed. Pepper lying in your spot. The blanket over him is the shape of a person.
- The feed moves: the camera slowly turns toward the sofa.
- On the camera's view, you see the sofa. You see the blanket. **You see your phone's glow under it.**
- **Cliff:** the SUSPICION meter fills. The bedroom door creaks open in the dark. Soft footsteps, two of them, not four.

### 5. THE ACT (7:45–9:00)
- Footsteps stop beside the sofa. You can't see it. You can hear it breathing like a person pretending to sleep.
- You do the only thing the rules allow: you pretend. You yawn. You say "hey buddy". You reach out and pet it.
- Under your hand: fur. Then not fur. Then fur again, as if it's deciding.
- It purrs. It lies down on your feet, like a cat. The meter falls a little.
- Morning. Everything is normal. Pepper wants breakfast.
- **The answer to the big question:** an email from the shelter. "We found the intake photos you asked for." Three
  photos of Pepper being dropped off. In each photo, the person holding the carrier is looking at the camera, smiling
  the same smile. Not one of them looks like the adopter on the forms.

### ENDING (9:00–9:40)
- "It's three eleven. You got up for water. Pepper is standing in the kitchen, facing you."
- "And this time he says your name."
- **NumberedChoice:** *1: SAY "HEY BUDDY"* / *2: RUN FOR THE DOOR*. Both cost something: pretend and you keep living with
  it; run, and you've stopped pretending.
- "Comment one or two. Your comments decide night two."
- **End card:** KEEP PRETENDING / NIGHT 2 / 3:11 AM / FOLLOW FOR NIGHT 2.

## Why this version might be stronger or weaker than SLOW BLINK

| | SLOW BLINK (protector cat) | KEEP PRETENDING (the cat is wrong) |
|---|---|---|
| Creepy cat behaviour | Standing up, staring, hissing, but for a reason | Standing up, walking on two legs, lying like a person, speaking: much creepier |
| Viewer instruction | "Close your eyes", real bonding tip | "Don't react", funny to try, very commentable |
| Emotion | Love for the cat; the ending protects him | Paranoia; the ending can't be trusted |
| Cat owners' reaction | Warm and scared ("my cat does this!") | Unsettled ("I'm looking at my cat differently") |
| Analog-horror trend | Weak | Strong: pet-cam night-vision footage carries whole scenes |
| New assets | Cat atlas, bed POV, eyelids | Cat atlas (plus upright walk), pet-cam UI filter, kitchen and sofa sets |

## Production notes

- **Pet cam is the cheapest scary device we have.** It's a UI layer (`CctvOverlay` / `CctvFilter` from `ns-ui.tsx`
  already exist) over pixel sets with a green-grey night-vision palette and white IR eyes. Scrubbing timestamps lets one
  set carry a whole chapter.
- **Cat atlas** (`pepper`): sit, loaf, walk0–3, sleep curl, headbutt, eat, look-back, **stand0–2**, **upright
  walk0–3** (too smooth and too straight: the creepiest thing in the film), lying "like a person" on the pillow.
- **Sets (240×135):** kitchen (fridge, counter), bedroom, sofa / living room (reuse `livingroom` plate cropped to 16:9 if
  it reads), shelter cage row.
- **Sound:** purr, footsteps (four-beat vs two-beat, so the ear tells you it's walking upright), the camera's motor
  whine when it turns, a phone-notification chime that becomes a dread motif.
- **The voice line** at the end ("your name") uses the narrator's own take with fx. Keep it as a whisper, not a monster
  voice.
