# Selling your soul — script draft v2

One rule, stated by the receipt: **every gift is paid for with a piece of you.**
v1 mixed two engines (the receipt's costs + coworkers vanishing) and viewers would have had to track both.
v2 drops the vanishing coworkers. The receipt is the only engine; the job twist comes out of it on Day 7.

Look: Pixel Dread, new supermarket world. Tone: dread with dark irony. Target ~88 s, ~215 words.
Ending: Take their place / Take theirs. Narrator: the user's voice (Chatterbox, user_ref.wav).

## How the viewer makes the connections

1. **Same ritual every day.** DAY card → the gift → the receipt prints (same sound every time) and the
   narrator reads `PAID WITH: ___` → the next shot shows the loss. By Day 2 the viewer knows the pattern
   and starts guessing the price before it prints.
2. **The cost is the ironic flip of the gift**, and both share one object on screen:
   - money → taste: the steak bought with the money tastes like paper
   - manager job → sleep: new manager badge on the nightstand, you awake all night
   - fame → Mom: the same phone that shows 2M followers shows Mom asking who you are
   - everyone obeys you → your hands don't: your own hands stacking shelves at 3 AM
3. **SOUL meter in the HUD**: 100% → 80 → 60 → 40 → 20, red flash on each payment. Day 5 (no refunds)
   it doesn't move. Day 7 it hits 0%.
4. **The receipt is a running ledger.** Each time it appears it's one line longer, so the viewer can
   read the whole week's bill at a glance.
5. **Day 6's strangers hold the same receipt prop.** No explanation needed: they signed too. On Day 7
   they're wearing the same uniform as you.
