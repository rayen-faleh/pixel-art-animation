# DON'T REPLY: Day 1 script draft v1

Short, 9:16, second person, present tense. Target ~80 s (~185 words at our 140 wpm).
Rule of the threat (never said outright): **it can't find you until you show it you're there.**
Read receipts, the typing bubble and the green "Active now" dot are the noise. HUD meter: **ONLINE**.
MrBeast is a name on screen only: no face, no logo, no brand art. Phone UI is generic (no real app).
